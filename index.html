Who we are

Not an agency.
A network.

Luxxor is an ecosystem built around assets, opportunities and relationships in the premium market. Owners, investors, brokers, concierges, family offices and partners meet at a single point of contact.

  • Access

    We create and manage access. An opportunity travels through the network before it ever becomes a listing.

  • Discretion

    Every property is presented by reference. Names, prices and terms stay between the parties involved.

  • Selection

    We don't publish everything we are shown. What we hold, we present ourselves, one property at a time.

Four verticals, one way of working. Luxxor Projects is the core of the ecosystem.

  • Projects Assets & investment
  • Vacation Private stays
  • Hotels Hospitality
  • Academy Internal training

Our markets run across Europe, the Middle East and the Americas.
